# Product Asset: Soulnio

## Basic Facts

- Product name: Soulnio
- Platform: web
- Product type: self-exploration web product with tarot, time compass, and relationship star network modules
- Visual style: soft mystical pink-purple mist, light glass surfaces, gentle glow, warm and ceremonial but bounded
- Main modules: Site Core, Tarot, Compass, Starnet, Journey

## What It Does

Soulnio gives users a quiet self-exploration space. It is not designed to make decisions for the user; it helps users pause, name feelings, see the shape of a question, and return choice to themselves.

Core capabilities found in the project:

- Home page introduces Soulnio as a quiet garden for tarot, time compass, and relationship star network
- About page explains the principle: do not seek answers outside, do not let tools decide for you, do not create fear
- Tarot module includes spreads, card draw flow, readings, records, favorites, card meaning pages, speech input, and share cards
- Compass module includes profile files, chart experience, depth / period choices, record details, and action rhythm suggestions
- Starnet module helps users place people into a relationship star network and read relationship-oriented reflection
- Today entry supports daily tarot or daily compass style entry points
- Legal copy frames the product as self-exploration and entertainment, not medical, legal, financial, or irreversible life advice

## Product Experience

Soulnio should feel like entering a soft digital divination garden. The experience is about slowing down, naming what is happening inside, and reading a warm but bounded reflection. It should not sound like a fortune-telling ad.

Tone for describing the product:

- Gentle, bounded, emotionally warm, precise
- Good framing: "a quiet place to organize a question"
- Good framing: "not asking a tool to decide, but using it to see yourself more clearly"
- Good framing: "tarot, time compass, and relationship star network as three different ways to pause"

## Target Users

- People who enjoy tarot, self-reflection, emotional journaling, and ritual-like digital experiences
- Users who feel mentally noisy and want a softer way to organize questions
- People interested in relationship reflection and personal rhythm
- Users who like beautiful, calm, browser-based experiences

## What To Emphasize

- Soulnio is about self-exploration, emotional organization, and reflection
- The product voice is warm but not absolute
- The product explicitly avoids fear-based and fatalistic claims
- The modules offer different entry points for different kinds of questions
- It is web-based and designed as a quiet, ceremonial experience

## Boundaries And Claims To Avoid

Do not claim unless later confirmed:

- Do not promise accurate fortune-telling, destiny prediction, reconciliation, wealth, health, or life-changing results
- Do not present outputs as medical, legal, financial, psychological, or irreversible life advice
- Do not pressure users with fear, fate, or certainty
- Do not say the product replaces therapy, consultation, or professional help
- Do not mention user numbers, revenue, rankings, or testimonials

Safe wording:

- "self-exploration"
- "helps organize questions"
- "offers a reflection"
- "returns the choice to the user"
- "for entertainment and personal reflection"
