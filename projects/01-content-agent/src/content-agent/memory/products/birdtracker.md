# Product Asset: BirdTracker iOS

## Basic Facts

- Product name: BirdTracker
- Platform: iOS
- Product type: mobile app for bird discovery, birding hotspots, and observation records
- Visual style: bright, soft, nature-oriented iOS interface with bird illustrations and glass-like cards
- Main tabs found in code: Atlas, Tracking, Planet, Camera, Honors
- Current code includes membership screens and profile / achievement-style areas

## What It Does

BirdTracker helps users explore birds, find observation places, and save sightings as a personal birding journey.

Core capabilities found in the code:

- Onboarding introduces bird discovery around the world
- Bird atlas with searchable and filterable bird entries
- Species detail pages with photos, sounds, facts, traits, colors, field marks, habitats, behavior, and range details
- Map-based Tracking view for birding hotspots and nearby birding places
- Location permission flow for showing nearby hotspots and recentering on current location
- Filters for range and time in the tracking experience
- Search for birding spots or bird species
- Camera flow for taking a photo and saving an observation record
- Recent observation records with species name, place, date, and local image
- Profile / Honors area with favorites, collected birds, photo count, progress, and achievements
- Live list and live player surfaces exist in the app structure
