# Product Asset: Math Puzzle

## Basic Facts

- Product name: Math Puzzle
- Product type: mobile-first number puzzle game / product prototype
- Core mechanic: fill numbers into crossed equations so every horizontal and vertical equation works
- Visual style: warm, clean, readable board with a slightly wooden / cozy puzzle feeling
- Current prototype includes a real CrossMath generation algorithm through crossmath-runtime.js

## What It Does

Math Puzzle gives users a calm number puzzle built around cross-shaped equations.

Core capabilities found in the project:

- Home screen with "New Game" and custom mode
- Custom mode for minimum value, maximum value, equation count, shape, and difficulty
- Game page with board, candidate number pool, hint, undo, clear, and reset
- Result modal showing time, hint count, and difficulty
- Settings drawer with validation, sound toggle placeholder, and warm background
- Continue / resume support in the store copy
- Adjustable number range, operators, equation count, and board shapes in store copy

## Product Experience

The user looks at a board where equations cross each other, then chooses numbers from a candidate pool and places them into blanks. The satisfaction comes from checking relationships, finding a clue, placing one number, and watching the whole board become clearer.

Tone for describing the product:

- Calm, clever, satisfying, not stressful
- Good framing: "a small math puzzle for short breaks"
- Good framing: "not a math exam, more like a clean little logic board"
- Good framing: "fill one number, and the board becomes easier to read"

## Target Users

- Users looking for short-session mobile games
- Students or adults who enjoy mental arithmetic without pressure
- Puzzle players who like adjustable difficulty
